I
1010 CEChola Dynasty

The Chola Genesis

Raja Raja Chola I commanded 60,000 artisans for 7 years. Granite quarried 60 km away. The 80-ton capstone hauled to 66m using a 6 km earthen ramp — no cranes, no mortar.
